What's Included
Everything a true
physician relationship
should be.

One annual fee, for every covered member. No per-visit charges. No portal maze. Hospital, specialist, and prescription costs remain with your insurance — membership pays for the relationship.

01
Direct Physician Access
Unlimited texting, calls, and video — seven days a week. Same- or next-day in-person visits for anything acute. After hours, you reach Dr. Lewis — not an answering service.
02
Annual Executive Physical
Ninety unhurried minutes. Head-to-toe exam, ECG as indicated, imaging coordination, and a full results review — in person, with Dr. Lewis. No portal message, no nurse callback, no timer.
03
100+ Biomarker Panel
Advanced lipids (ApoB, Lp(a), NMR), hormones, inflammation (hs-CRP, IL-6), micronutrients, heavy metals, gut markers, and early-cancer signals — via Function Health, annually.
04
Annual Health Blueprint
A written, physician-authored year-ahead strategy. Lab trends, lifestyle, family history, and risks distilled into clear priorities — nutrition, movement, screening, specialist plan, twelve-month objectives.
05
Continuous Monitoring
Your wearable (Apple Watch, Oura, compatible) feeds into Heads Up Health with personal thresholds. When the numbers shift, Dr. Lewis calls you — before you call him.
06
Warm Specialist Referrals & Care Coordination
Cardiology, orthopedics, dermatology, psychiatry, and more. Dr. Lewis calls physician-to-physician before your appointment. Hospital admissions, acute incidents, and travel prep — handled personally.
